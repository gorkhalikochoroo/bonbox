# BonBox — Positioning & Pitch (one-pager)

*Last updated: 2026-05-30. Source: trust / save-money / grow-business brainstorm. DK-first.*

---

## Who it's for
Danish SMB **restaurants & cafés** (esp. 1–2 locations) drowning in separate vendors and midnight admin. Generalises to other DK small businesses, but the restaurant is the design driver.

## What category we're in (and aren't)
BonBox is the **AI back-office brain** for the business — the layer *above* the POS that sees everything.

- **Not a POS** — we don't ring up orders or run tabs (we sit on top of whatever till they use).
- **Not OpenTable** — we don't sell a diner marketplace; we turn *their own* audience into bookings.
- **Not a replacement revisor** — the revisor still signs off; we do the grunt work in between.

## The one-liner
> **BonBox is the back-office brain that pays for itself on day one with the tools it replaces, keeps your revisor happy with closing-ready books, and fills your empty Tuesday — because it's the only thing that sees your whole restaurant.**

## Why us — the moat (say this once, mean it)
Every rival owns **one slice**: DinnerBooking has *demand*, DataLøn has *cost*, the POS has *revenue*, e-conomic has the *ledger*, the revisor has all of it but *monthly and by hand*. BonBox is the only system that holds **demand × cost × revenue × cash together, daily**. A feature can be copied; a missing data input means *becoming a different company*. That "whole-business graph" is what makes everything below uniquely possible.

## The three pillars

### 1. Trust — "the revisor's favourite tool"
The buyer is managing fear (SKAT, payroll mistakes, looking sloppy). We win by reducing the *felt risk of letting us near the money*, not with features.

- **Reframe, don't compete:** the revisor still signs off → BonBox just makes their bill smaller and the month-end painless.
- **Prepare, you approve — never silent-file.** The approval gate *is* the trust feature. Every number drills to its source (e.g. "this MOMS figure = these 142 bilag → bank → Z-reports").
- **Proof stack:** accountant-grade artifacts (bilagsnummer, doc-hash, signature, Bogføringsloven §10, provenance footer) · read-only revisor login · MitID · **read-only** bank access via a licensed AISP (we can't move your money) · GDPR/Art.9 + downloadable DPA · DK company + CVR + Danish support · **one-click export / no lock-in** · founding price + a real free tier.
- **Killer line:** *"Closing-ready books your revisor will thank you for."*
- **Highest-leverage channel:** get **revisorer to recommend it** to their own clients. If the trusted human says "put it in BonBox," the #1 objection flips into the #1 acquisition channel.

### 2. Saves money — "it pays for itself before you change a thing"
At **~249 kr/mo (founding Pro)**, the *hard, zero-behaviour-change* levers alone — replaced subscriptions (~450) + a lighter revisor bill (~600) + the founding lock (~100) ≈ **1,150 kr/mo** — already pay it back **~4.6×**. Conservatively all-in **~3,700 kr/mo (~15×)**; ~8,000 in a good month.

- **Killer line:** *"The subscriptions we replace already cover our price. Everything after that — your revisor bill, your slow-shift labour, your no-shows — is profit we hand back."*
- **Lead with the hard payback** (survives any skeptic), then labour-% as *"and here's the big one — if you act on it."*
- **Honest:** consolidation only saves if they cancel the old tools (make it a 1-tap onboarding step); revisor savings land at contract renewal; the biggest lever (labour) needs the owner to act; no-show "recovery" is retained *margin*, not gross revenue — never headline the gross.

### 3. Grows the business — "we fill your empty Tuesday"
Beyond saving — the one story a café owner *needs*:

> **"BonBox knows you'll be empty on Tuesday before you do — and fills the tables for you."**

It watches forward booking pace against *that café's own normal*, flags the gap by Tuesday 9am, and hands over the fix in one tap: *"Tonight's 25% light — send the open 18:00 slot to these 14 regulars we haven't seen since March? They've consented. [Send]."*

- It's the rare lever that's **pure incremental revenue** (empty tables earn zero), **effortless**, and **impossible for anyone else** (needs forward demand × guest history × consent × your baseline).
- Realistic engaged-venue upside ≈ **3–6% of revenue** (~15–40k kr/mo on a 5–8M venue), with fill-empty-tables the largest, cleanest share.

## Pricing story
Free (taste) → **Starter 199** (founding 129) → **Pro 349** (founding 249). The free tier lets them *feel* a "wow" before paying; the founding rate is a because-reason ("founding members lock this in"). Anchor price against the **revisor invoice + lost hours**, never against other apps.

## The 3 first-week trust moments (engineer these)
1. **"It caught the 740 kr till shortage"** — Z-report vs expected cash, first night.
2. **"It reconciled itself"** — 9am brief: *"everything matched except 1 item — tap to review."*
3. **"The MOMS number's already there, with the receipts behind it"** — deadline *handled*, not just reminded.

Each is specific, verifiable, about *their* money, and zero-effort. Skeptics don't trust dashboards — they trust being caught right about their own cash.

## Honest boundaries (where we don't win — and shouldn't pretend to)
- **In-service ops** (tabs, kitchen-fire, bill-split) → a full POS wins. We're the brain, not the till.
- **Diner-discovery marketplace** → OpenTable/TheFork send *new* diners (for a fee); we convert *your* traffic cheaply.
- **The owner who loves their 800-kr/mo family-friend revisor** → don't fight it. Win the *mess between visits*.
- **"Files itself"** (MOMS/eIndkomst) stays **one-tap-with-a-stop-window**, never silent — regulatory liability + honest-claims doctrine.

## The whole-graph guardrail
The moat is only as strong as two inputs: the **daily close** (revenue truth) and **reservations** (demand). Protect those above all — they're the fuel for Labour-% and fill-empty-tables.
